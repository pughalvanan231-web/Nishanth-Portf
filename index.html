What software and toolset do you edit with?

All projects are edited and mastered using industry-standard applications: Adobe Premiere Pro for offline editing, Adobe After Effects for motion graphics and compositing, DaVinci Resolve Studio for professional color grading, and CapCut for hyper-retention vertical formats.

What is your typical turnaround time per project?

Turnaround depends on project scope. First drafts for short-form content (Reels/TikTok/Shorts) are typically delivered within 24 to 48 hours. Long-form YouTube videos, commercials, and brand films generally take 3 to 5 business days.

How does the revision and review process work?

We streamline client feedback via Frame.io and timestamped review links. You can leave frame-accurate notes, draw annotations, and request changes directly on the timeline. Every project includes complimentary revision rounds to ensure the final master meets your exact creative vision.

What resolutions and delivery formats do you provide?

We deliver high-bitrate master files in 4K UHD or 1080p (ProRes, H.264, or H.265). We provide standard 16:9 widescreen exports for YouTube and broadcast, alongside 9:16 vertical versions optimized for mobile platforms.

How do we get started working together?

Getting started is simple: click "Hire Me" or email your project brief along with cloud footage links (Google Drive, Dropbox, or WeTransfer), reference videos, and delivery deadlines. We will review the scope and start the rough cut immediately.
